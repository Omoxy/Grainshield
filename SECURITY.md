# Security Policy

## Reporting Security Issues

GrainShield takes the safety of farmer records, financial flows, and platform infrastructure seriously.

If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability, please report it responsibly.

### Contact

Please send an email to security@grainshield.co.ke with details of your finding.

For encrypted reports, please request our PGP public key via the security contact email.

### Scope

The following services and repositories are within scope:

* GrainShield Web Application (Next.js dashboard)
* GrainShield USSD Gateway and Session Handler
* Backend Microservices: farmer, policy, payment, price, risk, warehouse, notification
* Infrastructure configurations and Kubernetes deployment manifests

The following activities are strictly out of scope:

* Denial of service attacks against live systems
* Physical attacks against warehouse depots, collection centers, or offices
* Social engineering of farmers, cooperative staff, or employees

### What to Include in Your Report

* Description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
* Steps to reproduce the issue, including requests or commands
* Relevant software versions, routes, or affected services
* Proof of concept code or screenshots, if available

### Response Timelines

* Initial receipt acknowledgment: within 24 hours
* Severity triage and validation: within 72 hours
* Critical fix release: within 7 calendar days
* Non critical fix release: within 30 calendar days

### Safe Harbor

Security researchers acting in good faith to discover and report vulnerabilities in accordance with this policy will not face legal action. We ask that you give our team reasonable time to remediate any issue before public disclosure.
